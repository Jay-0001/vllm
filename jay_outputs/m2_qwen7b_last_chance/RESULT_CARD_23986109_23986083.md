# Qwen7B Q/K RoPE oracle: completed region screen

## Decision

The combined Q/K kernel is a real, semantics-matched physical alternative on
the tested BF16 inputs. It reduces two Inductor launches to one. It wins on
decode token counts 1 and 4, but loses on prefill counts 512 and 2048. This
is a shape-dependent **region** result, not a measured model or serving win.
M2 remains a candidate; its downstream and compiler-generalization gates are
open.

## Provenance and correctness

Jobs `23986109` and `23986083` used Git
`9f572081fbd7d4ebe403c7caeb13697dfedf922d`, PyTorch `2.14.0+cu132`,
Triton `3.8.0`, and one A100-SXM4-40GB each. All four cases completed.
Generated Inductor wrappers list two Q/K RoPE `.run(...)` calls at every
shape; the combined kernel has one Triton launch. Q, K, and V matched
Inductor exactly for the single seeded input at each shape (reported maximum
absolute error 0). Inductor versus eager vLLM differed by at most 0.015625
for decode and 0.03125 for prefill, within the script's tolerance.

## CUDA graph replay region times

Values are microseconds per Q/K region call. Each arm has two measurements
from one job; percentages pair the measurements in order. Positive means
the combined kernel is faster.

| Tokens | Inductor samples | Combined samples | Combined change |
| ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 1 | 3.219, 3.212 | 1.573, 1.568 | 51.1%, 51.2% faster |
| 4 | 3.607, 3.599 | 1.641, 1.638 | 54.5%, 54.5% faster |
| 512 | 10.258, 7.909 | 13.296, 10.333 | 29.6%, 30.6% slower |
| 2048 | 33.240, 29.272 | 39.329, 36.741 | 18.3%, 25.5% slower |

The prefill arms drifted substantially between their two blocks. Both paired
blocks still put the combined kernel behind. No profiler counters establish
the cause of its prefill loss; launch count alone explains neither throughput
nor occupancy. CUDA graph replay measures this isolated GPU region and does
not reproduce the full vLLM request path, where CUDA graphs were disabled in
the prior compile dump.

## Thesis gate

The narrow Q/K candidate passes the two-shape decode region threshold within
one run, but independent replication, edge-case correctness, and integration
into the deployed compiled graph are still required. The present result does
not show that Inductor's **downstream model performance** is worse. It also
does not yet show a general fusion-boundary selection rule: this is one
Qwen-specific RoPE fan-out. A controlled full-model comparison is warranted
only after the region win replicates and the implementation preserves graph
outputs, attention inputs, and compile/capture behavior. The prefill crossover
argues against an unconditional combined-kernel substitution.
